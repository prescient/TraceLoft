# Bag mapping / gapping

Practice → Full swing → Bag mapping. Choose a bag, clubs, carry/total and a sample goal; launch live
or an explicitly synthetic demo. Switch clubs using the existing direct picker or the map's Select
buttons. The plan retains bag/club identity and names. Finish explicitly; sample completion never
stops a session unexpectedly. Missing distances do not fill the sample; excluded/removed shots do not
contribute. Additional clubs may be recorded but only planned clubs enter this map.

The map uses all included session shots for the planned clubs, independent of workspace filters.
Median and inclusive/type-7 Q1/Q3 form the display; IQR is Q3−Q1 and needs two readings. Clubs sort by
median distance; gaps compare neighboring sampled clubs, and overlapping IQRs are labeled. This is
descriptive sample spread, not a confidence interval. Source distances remain distinct from flight
estimates. Export bag map CSV or the range's filtered shot CSV. Clean up shots with the shared
workspace; corrections, exclusions and Undo immediately update the map without rewriting raw data.

Saved sessions retain the collection plan in the existing SQLite payload. Review, abandon, recoverable
delete/restore and capture ownership follow the range lifecycle. Multi-session comparisons belong to
Analyze. Numeric full-swing capture and physical iPad acceptance remain pending.
